*Effective December 16, 2024. This policy may be updated occasionally.*

## Guarantee of confidentiality

At King Mufasa Expeditions, we value and respect your privacy. This policy aims to provide confidence when using our website.

## What we collect, and why

When you contact King Mufasa Expeditions via our website, we may collect personal information including your name, phone number, and email address. This information is needed to provide quotations or share brochures. We do not share your information with third parties except as required by law or for operational purposes, such as communicating booking details to suppliers.

We may use your email address for marketing purposes, including promotions aligned with your interests. You can opt out at any time by selecting the appropriate option on our Contact Us form, or by contacting us directly.

## Data security

Protecting your personal data is a priority for us. Information is encrypted using SSL technology before transmission, and our systems are protected by firewalls. Website visit data is collected to enhance user experience and improve our services, and is used for internal reporting without identifying individuals.

## Cookie usage

Our website uses cookies to improve functionality and analyze traffic. Cookies are small files stored on your device that help us understand how you interact with our site. They do not contain personal data and cannot harm your device. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

## Visitor activity monitoring

We use Google Analytics to track visitor activity, including page visits and interactions. This data helps us improve the website and our marketing strategies. Google Analytics collects IP addresses without personal identification. You can disable cookies to prevent this tracking.

## External links

Our website includes links to other sites which have their own privacy policies. King Mufasa Expeditions is not responsible for external sites' practices, or for information you share with them.

## Questions and modifications

If you have questions about our privacy practices or wish to modify your consent, please contact us at [info@kingmufasaexpeditions.com](mailto:info@kingmufasaexpeditions.com).
