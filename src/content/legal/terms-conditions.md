## A. Making a booking

Booking a safari or tour is often an interactive process. After you find us and have an idea of what you want to do, we'll communicate back and forth on the tour, the recommended package, and timing — this is normal, and ensures you book the right trip.

Once we've agreed a quote and you're ready to proceed, we'll send payment details for a deposit, then confirmation once it's received.

We'll then send forms to collect details such as inbound flights and dietary restrictions. Our Registration Form permits certain limited special requests to be entered into the contract — such as dietary restrictions — and we'll do our best to honour them, communicating in advance if we can't.

A request made outside our Registration Form does not form part of the contract, regardless of what other communication may have passed. At any point between booking and delivery of receipt paperwork, the Company reserves the right to decline to supply the trip.

## B. Price guarantee

Once your deposit is received, we guarantee to honour every price on your quotation, with these exceptions:

- **Estimated price increases** — where we book more than a year ahead and don't yet have final supplier prices, we include an estimate. It's reassessed near final payment, and you're obliged to cover increases up to twice the original estimate.
- **Government-regulated fees and taxes** — sudden changes to park fees or taxes in Tanzania are your responsibility.
- **Fuel surcharges** — passed on only if our suppliers demand them of us.
- **Major errors** — if we've significantly undercharged due to an error in our quotation, we reserve the right to withdraw the offer.
- **Exchange rate fluctuations** — rare supplier-side surcharges are your responsibility.

## Payment terms

- Camping safari deposit: 25%
- Lodge safari deposit: 40%
- Tailor-made bookings: 10%–70%, on request
- Final payment can be made on arrival (cash accepted and preferred)

Payment can be made by bank transfer, credit card, or online payment (e.g. PesaPal); we'll provide banking details. All payments go to company accounts in Tanzania — we hold no offshore accounts.

Groups may pay individually, but all communication should run through the group leader, who remains financially responsible for the whole trip.

## Payment and invoices

All instalments must be paid before your tour start date. If paying by bank transfer, please email us the receipt — we'll confirm by return. Card and online payments carry a 3.5% surcharge; wire transfer charges also apply.

## Currency

Quotations are made in US$, Euros, or Tanzanian shillings. Cash is accepted in US$ preferably, though Euros and Pound sterling are also accepted, alongside direct bank transfer.

## Tour element modifications

**By the Company.** We make every effort to run the itinerary as quoted, but reserve the right to make changes for your suitability. Delays or alterations caused by flight delays, weather, strikes, civil unrest, or other events beyond our control are the client's responsibility to insure against.

For group tours, we may reorder (not shorten) the itinerary to accommodate other group members. If a trip element becomes unavailable — a lodge damaged by fire or flood, for instance — all payment obligations for that element are waived, and we'll seek your agreement on any change with a cost impact. Minor modifications that don't affect price or duration — swapping up to two interchangeable lodges, reordering nights, adjusting nights per lodge, or changing a transport supplier — don't require your separate agreement.

**By the Client.** Changes requested after deposit is paid usually carry an amendment fee to cover admin costs; cancelling any element attracts standard cancellation charges. Additions to an existing booking usually carry no extra fee.

## Cancellation

Cancelling less than 10 days before the tour forfeits the tour fee, except in extraordinary, provable circumstances. Cancelling more than 10 days out gets a refund (less bank costs), except for lodge-based tours, which follow the lodge's own policy. Exchanges are allowed; no refunds are given for cancelled individual tours. Full travel and flight insurance is required.

**By the Company.** In rare cases — supplier failure, a regulatory change, road or border closure, extreme weather — we'll substitute an equivalent service where possible, or seek your agreement to a bigger change. We reserve the right to cancel a trip before departure for reasons of safety (terrorism, natural disaster, political instability), refunding all land payments received, which releases us from further liability. We're not responsible for incidental costs such as visa fees, vaccinations, or non-refundable flights.

## Insurance

Travel and medical insurance is required for all participants, covering cancellation, medical expenses, evacuation and repatriation, and loss or theft of belongings. All optional activities — climbing, diving, water sports, horse riding — are undertaken at your own risk; check that your policy covers high-risk activities before you travel.

## Tour suitability and behaviour

We may terminate, without notice, the arrangements of any customer whose behaviour risks distress, damage, danger, or illegality to any third party, themselves, or property. You must disclose accurate health and mobility information at booking, and update us on any change before the trip starts. Where a customer is judged unable to cope with a trip's demands, our obligations cease, full cancellation charges apply, and no refund is due.

## Problems during a trip

Raise any problem with the Company, your guide, or our agents as soon as it happens, so we can act on it. Complaints raised only after the trip, with no chance for us to correct them at the time, will not be entertained. Photographic evidence gathered at the time is the most useful thing you can bring to a post-trip claim.

We have no separate liability to you for these events, though sums you recover from the airline may be offset against any obligation we might otherwise owe you as tour operator.

Bookings made less than seven days in advance carry this risk entirely at the customer's own risk, given how little slack Tanzania's logistics allow for last-minute changes.

Vaccination requirements change; check with a local travel health specialist or [the CDC's travel site](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/travel). Tanzania does not always match the hygiene, security, or service standards of more developed regions — take sensible precautions, and raise any specific concerns with us before you travel.
