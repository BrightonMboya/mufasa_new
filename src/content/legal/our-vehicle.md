At King Mufasa Expeditions, your comfort and safety are our top priority. We use strong, reliable safari vehicles to make sure you enjoy a smooth and unforgettable experience.

We operate 4WD Toyota Land Cruisers, specially prepared for African safari conditions — large windows for wildlife viewing, a pop-up roof for photography, and plenty of legroom.

## Equipment included

- Cooler/fridge for refreshments
- Binoculars for wildlife spotting
- Inverter system to charge phones and tablets
- Reference books on African flora and fauna

## Safety features

- HF radios connecting to our office and other safari vehicles
- First aid kits
- Fire extinguishers
- Tool kits and spare tyres

Each vehicle accommodates a maximum of 7 guests, and undergoes rigorous maintenance with checks after every safari.

## Vehicle amenities summary

- Pop-up roof
- Window seat for every guest
- Refrigerator
- Luggage space
- Inverter charging system
- Binoculars included

Luggage space is limited; we recommend storing non-essential items at our Arusha office for the duration of your safari.
