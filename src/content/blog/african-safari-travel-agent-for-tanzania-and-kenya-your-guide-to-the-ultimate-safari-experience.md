## Top Safari Destinations in Tanzania and Kenya

### Tanzania Safari Highlights

Tanzania is known for vast landscapes and iconic wildlife experiences.

Popular destinations include:

- **Serengeti National Park** – Famous for the Great Migration
- **Ngorongoro Crater** – Dense wildlife in a unique volcanic caldera
- **Tarangire National Park** – Large elephant herds and baobab trees
- **Lake Manyara National Park** – Tree-climbing lions and birdlife

A knowledgeable African safari travel agent helps you choose the best parks based on season and interests.

## Kenya Safari Highlights

Kenya offers classic safari scenery and excellent wildlife viewing.

Top destinations include:

- **Maasai Mara National Reserve** – Big cats and dramatic river crossings
- **Amboseli National Park** – Elephants with Mount Kilimanjaro views
- **Lake Nakuru National Park** – Flamingos and rhino conservation
- **Tsavo East and West** – Diverse landscapes and fewer crowds

Kenya is ideal for first-time safari travelers and photographers.

## Best Time to Visit Tanzania and Kenya for Safari

Timing plays a major role in safari success.

- **June to October** – Best wildlife viewing and Great Migration
- **January to March** – Calving season and fewer crowds
- **April to May** – Green season with lower prices
