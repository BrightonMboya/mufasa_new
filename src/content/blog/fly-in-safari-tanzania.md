Tanzania is a large country with many beautiful national parks spread across different regions. Traveling by road to these parks, especially some remote ones like Serengeti, can be tiring and sometimes uncomfortable due to long, bumpy, and dusty paths. However, there's a much easier way to explore the parks by flying. With Tanzania's fly-in safaris, you can skip the long drives and fly directly into the national park of your choice. After a short flight, you can start your game drives and enjoy the wildlife right away. You can even fly out to the city, visit Zanzibar Islands, or explore the coast of Tanzania after your safari. Fly-in safaris provide a quicker and more convenient way to experience the best of Tanzania's wildlife and landscapes.

## Fly-in Safari, Flight Options

Some local charter flight companies offer scheduled flights to and from the national park airstrips, with daily flights on popular routes. For certain routes, a minimum of two seats may be required to confirm the flight. Alternatively, if you prefer more flexibility, you can hire a private charter flight for yourself or a small group, offering a more personalized and convenient travel experience.

## Why Fly In & Out

- If you have limited time and want to make the most of the places you visit.
- If you want to avoid long, bumpy drives and get to your destination more comfortably and quickly.
- If you're looking for a stress-free, convenient travel option that lets you enjoy Tanzania's beauty without the hassle of long road journeys.
- If you want to make the most of your trip, allowing more time for wildlife viewing and relaxation rather than being stuck in transit.

## Optimum Fly-in Safari, Tanzania

The best way to make the most of fly-in safaris in Tanzania is by combining them with your game drives, so you don't have to spend time driving long distances back. For example, if you're visiting the Serengeti after Ngorongoro, instead of driving all the way back to Ngorongoro or Arusha, you can choose to fly directly from the Serengeti to Zanzibar. This way, you save time and avoid long, tiring drives, allowing you to enjoy more of your safari experience and easily move between destinations. It's a convenient and efficient way to explore the country without the hassle of unnecessary travel.

## Where You Can Fly From (Start Your Fly-In Safari)

You are to arrive in Tanzania from 3 major international airports and from them, you can take a charter flight to a national park, starting your fly-in safaris:

- Zanzibar is one of the most sought destinations in Tanzania and a tourist hub you can fly to and from most national parks
- Dar es Salaam, being the financial hub & a ferry away from Zanzibar, from here you can get flights to all working airports and airstrips in Tanzania
- Kilimanjaro airport is the closest international airport to the Tanzania northern circuit parks. From this airport, you can get charter flights to the northern parks
- Arusha, being Tanzania's safari hub, is indeed a hub popular with those visiting Tanzania's famous northern parks. You can get charter flights to all northern parks, Serengeti, Manyara & Tarangire national parks
- Mwanza is an airport in a port city & Tanzania's second largest city by economic size from this airport you can get a charter flight to popular parks like the Serengeti
- From national parks to national parks, you can also fly from park to park. Example you can fly from Manyara national parks to Serengeti national park to Selous national park.

**Note:** these scheduled charter flights do run round trips & direct trips to & from the parks so your logistics should be keenly maintained.
