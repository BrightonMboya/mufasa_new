## Best Time to Find a Safari

One of the most common questions we get is: When is the best time to go on safari?

The truth is, there's no single best time—it depends on what you want to see.

- Dry season (June–October): Excellent wildlife viewing and the Great Migration
- Green season (November–March): Fewer crowds, lush landscapes, and great photography

King Mufasa Expeditions helps you find a safari at the perfect time for your goals.
