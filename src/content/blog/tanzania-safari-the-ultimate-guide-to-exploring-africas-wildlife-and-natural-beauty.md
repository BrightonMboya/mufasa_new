## Best National Parks to Visit on a Tanzania Safari

Tanzania has several outstanding national parks and conservation areas. Each destination offers a different wildlife experience, making it possible to create an itinerary that matches your interests and travel schedule.

### 1. Serengeti National Park

Serengeti National Park is one of the most famous safari destinations in the world and a highlight of any Tanzania safari.

Its endless grasslands support an incredible variety of wildlife, including lions, leopards, cheetahs, elephants, giraffes, zebras, buffalo, and many other species.

The Serengeti is particularly famous for the **Great Wildebeest Migration**, during which millions of wildebeest and zebras move through the Serengeti-Mara ecosystem in search of fresh grazing.

Depending on the season and location, visitors may witness large herds crossing the plains, the calving season, or dramatic river crossings.

A safari in Serengeti offers opportunities for morning, afternoon, and full-day game drives, allowing visitors to explore different areas of the park.

For travelers who want to experience Tanzania's wildlife at its best, Serengeti deserves a place on their itinerary.

### 2. Ngorongoro Crater

![A guide pointing out the landscape to a traveler on a highland viewpoint above the plains](/media/journeys/crater-view-with-guide.webp)

The Ngorongoro Crater is one of Tanzania's most remarkable natural attractions and a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Formed by the collapse of a large volcanic mountain, the crater contains a rich ecosystem that supports an impressive concentration of wildlife.

During a crater game drive, visitors may encounter lions, elephants, buffalo, zebras, hippos, hyenas, flamingos, and the endangered black rhinoceros.

The relatively concentrated wildlife population makes Ngorongoro an excellent destination for travelers who want rewarding wildlife viewing within a single day.

The surrounding highlands also provide beautiful scenery and opportunities to learn about local Maasai culture.

### 3. Tarangire National Park

![Safari guests in an open vehicle watching a large tusked elephant at close range](/media/journeys/elephant-watching-from-vehicle.webp)

Tarangire National Park is particularly famous for its large elephant populations and magnificent baobab trees.

During the dry season, the Tarangire River attracts wildlife from surrounding areas, creating excellent opportunities for game viewing.

Visitors can encounter elephants, giraffes, zebras, wildebeest, lions, leopards, buffalo, and numerous bird species.

Tarangire is an excellent destination for travelers looking for a quieter safari experience with beautiful landscapes and impressive wildlife.

It is also conveniently located near Arusha, making it a popular addition to northern Tanzania safari itineraries.

### 4. Lake Manyara National Park

Lake Manyara National Park offers a beautiful combination of forests, open grasslands, and lake environments.

The park is known for its elephants, baboons, giraffes, hippos, and diverse birdlife.

It is also traditionally associated with tree-climbing lions, although sightings are not guaranteed.

Lake Manyara is an excellent destination for travelers who want to experience different landscapes and wildlife habitats during their Tanzania safari.

### 5. Nyerere National Park

For travelers interested in exploring southern Tanzania, Nyerere National Park offers a different type of safari experience.

The park features extensive wilderness, rivers, woodland, and open plains.

Wildlife includes elephants, lions, giraffes, buffalo, hippos, crocodiles, wild dogs, and many bird species.

Depending on the area and lodge, visitors can enjoy traditional game drives, boat safaris, and guided walking safaris.

Nyerere is an excellent choice for travelers seeking a less conventional safari experience away from some of the busier northern safari routes.

## The Great Wildebeest Migration in Tanzania

![Travelers on foot with their guides, watching the wildebeest herds spread across the Serengeti](/media/journeys/travellers-watching-migration.webp)

One of the biggest attractions of a Tanzania safari is the Great Wildebeest Migration.

This extraordinary natural event involves the movement of approximately 1.5 million wildebeest, accompanied by hundreds of thousands of zebras and gazelles, across the Serengeti-Mara ecosystem.

The animals move in search of fresh grass and water, following seasonal rainfall patterns.

Although the migration takes place throughout the year, different periods offer different experiences.

### January to March: Calving Season

The southern Serengeti and Ndutu area become important locations during the calving season. Thousands of wildebeest calves are born during this period, attracting predators such as lions, cheetahs, and hyenas.

### April to May: The Green Season

Large herds begin moving north and west as rainfall patterns change. The landscape becomes green, creating beautiful scenery and excellent photography opportunities.

### June to July: Western Corridor

The migration progresses towards the western Serengeti, where visitors may have opportunities to witness the Grumeti River crossings.

### August to October: Northern Serengeti

Large herds move through the northern Serengeti, where dramatic Mara River crossings may occur.

### November to December: Return to the Southern Plains

As seasonal rains return, many herds move south towards the Serengeti plains.

The migration is a natural event, so exact locations and dates can change. Choosing the right safari dates and accommodation location is important when planning a migration-focused holiday.

## What Is the Best Time to Visit Tanzania for a Safari?

Tanzania is a year-round safari destination, but the best time to visit depends on the wildlife experiences you want to enjoy.

### June to October – Dry Season

This is one of the most popular periods for wildlife viewing. Vegetation is generally thinner, and animals often gather around water sources, making them easier to spot.

### January to March – Calving Season

This period is particularly rewarding for travelers interested in the wildebeest calving season in the southern Serengeti.

### April to May – Long Rainy Season

The landscape becomes green and beautiful. Some camps and roads may be affected by rainfall, but travelers can enjoy fewer crowds and excellent photographic scenery.

### November to December – Short Rainy Season

Short rains usually bring fresh vegetation and beautiful landscapes. Wildlife viewing can still be excellent, depending on the destination and weather conditions.

There is no single best month for every traveler. Your ideal travel period depends on your preferred destinations, budget, and interests.
