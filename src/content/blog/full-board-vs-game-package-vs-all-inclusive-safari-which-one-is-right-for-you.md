Planning a safari is an exciting endeavour, but choosing the right type of package can feel overwhelming. There are several factors that will influence your decision — are you travelling alone, as a couple or as a family? Should you opt for Full Board, Game Package, or All-Inclusive? This guide will break down the differences, inclusions, exclusions, pros, cons, and who should book each option to help you make an informed choice.

## Summary: Full Board vs Game Package vs All-Inclusive

| Feature | Full Board | Game Package | All-Inclusive |
|---------|-----------|--------------|---------------|
| Meals | Included (breakfast, lunch, dinner) | Included (breakfast, lunch, dinner) | Included (All meals & snacks) |
| Game Drives (camp vehicles) | Not included | Included | Included |
| Park Fees | Sometimes included | Sometimes included | Usually included |
| Transfers | Not usually included | Often included | Included |
| Drinks | Sometimes included | Often included | Included (alcoholic & non-alcoholic) |
| Extra Activities | Not included | Limited to safari-related | Broad range included |
| Customisation | High | Low | High |
| Ideal For | Independent travellers, flexible plans | First-time travellers looking for easy planning | Luxury travellers, stress-free planners |

## What is a Full Board Safari?

**Full Board** is typically associated with driving safaris. On a full board safari, travellers have their own dedicated guide and vehicle, and game drives are conducted using this arrangement. While meals are included, most camp-based activities need to be booked separately.

### Inclusions:

- Three daily meals
- Accommodation
- A dedicated vehicle and guide

### Exclusions:

- Camp-based activities (e.g., walking safaris, night game drives)
- Park fees and conservation levies (in some cases)
- A vehicle (guests use their own vehicle or hire a vehicle)

### Pros:

- Great flexibility for independent planning
- Personalised experience with a dedicated guide
- Ideal for combining multiple destinations

### Cons:

- Additional costs for camp activities
- Requires more planning and coordination

### Best For:

- Independent travellers who enjoy flexibility
- Groups or families wanting exclusive use of a guide and vehicle
- Families with kids, as Full Board allows for a personalised itinerary

## What is a Game Package Safari?

A **Game Package** is often part of a flying safari. Guests use the camp's vehicles and guides, and most camp-based activities are included, offering a hassle-free experience.

### Inclusions:

- Camp-based activities (e.g., game drives, walking safaris)
- Use of the camp's guides and vehicles
- All meals and accommodation
- Limited drinks

### Exclusions:

- Park fees and conservation fees (sometimes)
- Transfers, apart from to and from the airstrip
- Additional activities

### Pros:

- Comprehensive safari experience with minimal planning required
- Access to expert local guides
- Suitable for first-time safari goers

### Cons:

- Less flexibility compared to Full Board
- Shared game drives with other guests (private vehicles sometimes available at an additional cost)

### Best For:

- First-time safari goers
- Travellers seeking convenience and ease

## What is an All-Inclusive Safari?

An All-Inclusive Safari covers virtually everything, including accommodations, meals, game drives, and even extras like beverages and park fees. It's the ultimate hassle-free option.

### Inclusions:

- All meals and beverages
- Park fees and conservation levies
- Game drives and other camp-based activities
- Transfer to and from the camp

### Exclusions:

- Very few, if any, additional costs
- Tipping is usually excluded

### Pros:

- Completely worry-free experience
- Luxury and comfort
- No hidden costs

### Cons:

- Higher upfront cost
- Less flexibility for customisation

### Best For:

- Honeymooners – Opt for All-Inclusive to enjoy luxury and romance without worrying about logistics.
- Travellers seeking a luxury experience

## Which Option Should You Choose?

Your choice depends on your travel style, budget, and the experience you're seeking.

### Choose Full Board If:

- You value flexibility and independence
- You're travelling with a group or family that prefers exclusive arrangements

### Choose Game Package If:

- You're a first-time safari-goer
- You want a well-rounded safari with minimal planning

### Choose All-Inclusive If:

- You're celebrating a special occasion, like a honeymoon
- You desire a seamless, luxury experience with no unexpected costs
