## Why Choose Tanzania for Your Safari Holiday?

Tanzania is home to some of the **most famous national parks and wildlife areas in the world**, making it a top choice for African safari tours.

## Highlights of Tanzania Safari Holidays

- Home of the **Great Migration** in Serengeti National Park
- The **Ngorongoro Crater**, a UNESCO World Heritage Site
- Large populations of lions, elephants, leopards, buffalo, and rhinos
- Beautiful landscapes including savannahs, lakes, and volcanic craters
- Friendly people and rich cultural experiences

A Tanzania safari holiday offers unmatched diversity in wildlife and scenery, all within one country.

## Top Destinations for Tanzania Safari Holidays

### Serengeti National Park

The Serengeti is world-famous for its endless plains and the Great Wildebeest Migration. It is one of the best places in Africa to see big cats and dramatic wildlife action.

### Ngorongoro Crater

Often called the "Garden of Eden," the Ngorongoro Crater offers incredible game viewing in a compact area, making it ideal for shorter safari holidays.

### Tarangire National Park

Known for its large elephant herds and iconic baobab trees, Tarangire is perfect for travelers who enjoy quieter parks with rich wildlife.

### Lake Manyara National Park

Famous for tree-climbing lions and birdlife, Lake Manyara is a great addition to any Tanzania safari itinerary.

## Best Time to Go on Tanzania Safari Holidays

- **June to October**: Best for wildlife viewing and the Great Migration
- **January to March**: Calving season in the Serengeti
- **November to May**: Fewer crowds, green landscapes, and excellent birdlife

Tanzania safari holidays are possible **year-round**, depending on your interests.
